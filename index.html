IGPOGP

QR-enabled inventory movements and gate pass automation

A practical example of turning an everyday operational requirement into a fully integrated ERP process.

We integrated QR code scanning with Oracle E-Business Suite to automate Inward Gate Pass (IGP) and Outward Gate Pass (OGP) processing. Instead of writing and re-typing details by hand, a single scan captures the transaction and records it directly in the ERP.

The same QR-based approach supports inventory movements. Receipts, issues and transfers between stores and locations are captured at the point of movement, so stock records stay current and match what is physically on the floor.

The result is faster processing, fewer manual entries and more accurate transactions, with clear traceability and tighter control over every movement of material in and out of your premises.
